I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Nicolaus Copernicus Astronomical Center (CAMK). My research focuses on gravitational lensing of gravitational waves from astrophysical sources such as merging binary black holes and rapidly spinning isolated neutron stars.

I work on developing analytical and numerical methods to study how gravitational lensing affects gravitational-wave signals and what we can learn about astrophysical sources and the Universe from these effects.

During my PhD, I also explored alternative theories of gravity and their observational signatures.

On this site you can find information about my research, publications, and academic activities.
